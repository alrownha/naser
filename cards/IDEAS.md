# Cricut Print Then Cut — formats and lessons for s.3d.ae giveaways

Learned from popular Cricut projects on the web (Design Space's own library is login-only and
can't be scraped; the same ideas are documented publicly). Use this as a menu when a new
giveaway or card is requested. All files still follow README.md: PNG only, white card,
transparent outside the cut line, ink-saving outlines, no helper text on the artwork.

## Formats that attach a 3D print to paper

| Format | Typical size | How the print attaches | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Backer / display card** (what we use for letter cards) | 9–10 × 14–15 cm | glued, or two slits cut by hand for a clip/strap | letter cards, coloring sets, any flat model |
| **Keychain header card** | 7 × 10 cm up to 9 × 14 cm; retail standard 3 × 5 in | one punch hole at the top (5–6 mm); the keyring clip goes through the hole so the model hangs on the card | keychains, mini figures with a ring |
| **Hang tag** | 5 × 9 cm, 6 × 10 cm | hole + ribbon/twine tied to the model | bag charms, thank-you tags |
| **Fold-over bag topper** | 6.5 × 2 in folded (i.e. 16.5 × 10 cm flat), fits a 3 in (7.5 cm) treat bag | folds over the bag's open edge, stapled at both ends | giveaway bag with the model + sweets inside |
| **Pencil / pen tag** | 12–13 × 19–20 cm | two slits ~8 cm apart, pencil slides through | the teacher coloring card |
| **Lollipop / stick holder** | ~9 × 18 cm shaped card | two small slits in the middle, stick goes through | lollipops, flag-on-stick, mini signs |
| **Bookmark** | 4.5–5 × 15 cm (1.8 × 6 in); magnetic: 5 × 11 cm folded | hole + tassel, or a flat model glued at the top | reading week, library events |
| **Round label / sticker** | 5–6 cm | printed on sticker paper, seals the bag | branding, event name, "from" |
| **Gift-card / pocket holder** | A6 folded | a pocket made by folding a flap and gluing the sides | vouchers, small flat prints |

## Design conventions seen across the popular projects
- One hero element (the 3D print or the big letter), everything else small and quiet.
- The hole is **centred, 8–10 mm from the top edge**, with ≥4 mm of paper around it.
- Rounded corners (3–6 mm) look finished and survive handling better than square ones.
- A thin dashed inner frame 4–6 mm inside the edge hides small cut offsets.
- Name/“from” line sits above the contact line; contact line is the smallest text.
- Bilingual: Arabic display font (Lalezar) for the Arabic title, a rounded Latin font
  (Baloo Bhaijaan 2) for the English — different fonts, same colour.
- Keep colour to 2 accents; for school giveaways navy + orange reads “space/science”,
  green + orange reads “nature”, pink + purple reads “thank you / celebration”.

## Production rules (Print Then Cut)
- Design Space A4 cut area ≈ 18.3 × 27 cm; A4 fits **2 cards of 10 × 15** or **4 of 9 × 13.3**.
- Upload as **Complex → Print Then Cut**; never erase the white (it is the card).
- Keep **Bleed on** when printing; it hides a 0.5–1 mm cut offset.
- **Calibrate** once (Menu → Calibration → Print Then Cut); recalibrate after Design Space updates.
- Print at **100% / actual size**; "fit to page" shifts the cut.
- Cardstock **65–80 lb (180–220 gsm)** on the LightGrip (blue) mat, pressed down with a brayer;
  setting "Medium Cardstock". Plain paper only for coloring sheets.
- The Cricut cuts only the **outer contour** of a Print Then Cut image. Interior slits and
  holes are made by hand (craft knife / hole punch), or added as separate cut shapes in
  Design Space and **Attached** to the image before Make It.
- Small cards: put 2–4 copies on one sheet in Design Space (Duplicate) to save paper.
